## About Normal Computing

[Normal Computing](https://www.normalcomputing.com/) is building a new paradigm of AI hardware to power human progress.

Normal makes inference servers and rack-scale systems designed to increase intelligence per watt for a future the world can afford to power.

Its thermodynamic compute-in-memory architecture reduces data movement so systems can deliver more inference within the same energy budget. The company uses AI to accelerate silicon co-design and product development.

Normal’s team has designed CPU and GPU IP at NVIDIA and Apple, built core TensorFlow frameworks, helped put AI into production at Google, and brought frontier technology to market.

The company’s primary offices are in New York City, London, and Mountain View.

Normal wanted customers evaluating it as a hardware supplier to have independent evidence of how its security controls worked in practice. Its own team owns and runs those controls day to day. To prepare for the company’s first SOC 2 Type 2 audit, Nicolas Martyanoff, Normal’s Platform Lead, brought in Probo to run the audit work around them.

## What Probo took on

Probo began work in January 2026. Its dedicated team drafted Normal’s policies, gathered evidence across HR and IT, and coordinated the audit from scoping through fieldwork.

In the months before the observation period, Probo identified the records the auditor would need to test Normal’s controls. It also helped Normal document who was responsible for onboarding and offboarding.

## A partner through the audit

MJD Advisors tested Normal’s controls over a three-month observation period beginning in July 2026. When fieldwork began in September, Probo became the auditor’s main point of contact.

> They prepared the evidence, handled the auditor and only came to us when they really needed our input, so the team could stay focused on building.

— **Nicolas Martyanoff, Platform Lead at Normal Computing**

## Built to keep running

Normal received its SOC 2 Type 2 report in November 2026, ten months after the work began. The company’s team continues to run the controls the auditor tested, from access reviews across its engineering and business systems to device encryption and vulnerability tracking. New risks are logged and followed up.

Probo’s work didn’t stop with the report. The team still meets with Normal regularly to keep policies, evidence and risks up to date as the company grows.

Customers and prospects can request Normal’s SOC 2 Type 2 report through its [compliance portal](https://compliance.normalcomputing.com/en).
