DORA asks financial entities to know exactly which critical functions depend on which ICT systems, vendors, and people. Until now, that mapping lived in spreadsheets.

The new [Business Functions register](/docs/product/assets-and-obligations) lets you define a critical ICT function once and link it to the assets, third parties, and people that keep it running. It's available in the console, and the same links are exposed through the CLI, MCP, and [n8n](/docs/developers/api/n8n/resources/business-function) if you'd rather manage it from code.
