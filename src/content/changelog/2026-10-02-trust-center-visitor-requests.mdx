Unlinking a document or audit from a trust center clears its [visitor access requests](/docs/product/compliance-portal/visitor-access-and-nda). The console asks for confirmation first, making the access change explicit before it happens.

Trust center files no longer have a hidden visibility setting. Delete a file when it should be removed instead of leaving it hidden.
