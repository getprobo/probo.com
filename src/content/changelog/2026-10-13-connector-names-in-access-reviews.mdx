When one provider has several connections, it should be obvious which one an access review is using.

Connector account names now appear on access-review source cards and in the accounts drawer. Campaigns preserve a snapshot of the name, so reviewers can still identify the connection used. Names already stored on an access source carry over, while CSV sources keep their own name.
