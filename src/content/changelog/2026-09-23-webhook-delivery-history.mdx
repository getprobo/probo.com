Webhook subscriptions now have a detail page for the endpoint, [subscribed event types](/docs/developers/api/webhooks/event-types), signing secret, and [delivery history](/docs/developers/api/webhooks/delivery-and-recovery). Filter deliveries by status, inspect or copy the outbound payload, and see the latest delivery from the subscription card.

Creating a subscription now has its own page, while delivery history is kept separate from the list of subscribed events. When a delivery needs debugging, the details are right where you expect them.
