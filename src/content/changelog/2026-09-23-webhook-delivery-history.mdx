Webhook subscriptions now have a detail page for the endpoint, subscribed event types, signing secret, and delivery history. Filter deliveries by status, inspect or copy the outbound payload, and see the latest delivery from the subscription card.

Creating a subscription now has its own page, while delivery history is kept separate from the list of subscribed events. When a delivery needs debugging, the details are right where you expect them.
