The [Probo MCP server](/docs/developers/api/mcp/overview) now works with Dust.

Add it as a remote MCP server in your Dust workspace using your Probo MCP URL (`https://eu.probo.com/api/mcp/v1` or `https://us.probo.com/api/mcp/v1`) and sign in with your Probo account. Your Dust agents can then read and update the same things you manage in the console: tasks, risks, documents, vendors, controls.

Agents only see what your Probo account can see. Permissions stay the same as in the console.
