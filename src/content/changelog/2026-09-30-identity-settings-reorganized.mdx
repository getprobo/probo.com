SAML and SCIM now start from separate settings sections. SAML configurations have dedicated create and edit pages, with Optional or Required enforcement chosen up front. The page shows verification status and the DNS TXT record alongside the configuration.

For provisioning, choose Google, Microsoft, or manual setup from provider cards. Each configured provider has its own page and a paginated event history in Relay.
