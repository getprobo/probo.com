[Cookie banner settings](/docs/product/cookie-banner/quickstart) now have separate Install, Configure, and Translations pages. Each page shows whether its work is draft, deactivated, or live.

Category edits open in a drawer on Configure. Tracker rows stay on the Trackers page, and theme controls now generate a [CSS snippet](/docs/product/cookie-banner/theming).
