The Probo [cookie consent banner](/products/cookie-banner) now speaks 9 additional languages.

Added: Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Turkish, Ukrainian, and Chinese.

That brings the total to 13 supported languages. If your product serves users across multiple regions, the banner now adapts to their browser language without any extra configuration on your end.

Combined with Probo's [regulation-aware consent mode](/docs/product/cookie-banner/geolocation) (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), this means visitors see a banner that is both localized and legally appropriate for their location. No patchwork of custom implementations needed.
