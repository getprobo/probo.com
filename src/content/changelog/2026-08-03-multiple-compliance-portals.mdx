Until now, every organization got one [compliance portal](/products/compliance-portal). If you served different customer segments, or ran separate audits for separate business units, everything lived on the same page.

Now you can run [multiple compliance portals](/docs/product/compliance-portal/multiple-portals) from one organization. Each portal gets its own catalog of audits, documents, and third parties, its own visitor experience, and its own publication controls. Pick which portal to manage from the console, and publish each one on its own schedule.
