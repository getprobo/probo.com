Task lists can now be narrowed to the person responsible. The assignee filter works for organization and measure tasks in the console, GraphQL, MCP, [CLI](/docs/developers/cli/commands/task), and [n8n](/docs/developers/api/n8n/resources/task).

Former members remain selectable, so old assignments do not disappear from view when someone leaves the team.
