description: "Employees can attach files to the tasks assigned to them and to their comments in the employee portal, with drag and drop or paste. Assigned employees can also edit the task description."
date: 2026-11-16
images: ["/changelog/2026-11-16-employee-portal-task-attachments-1.png"]
tags: ["Governance"]
---

Employees working on an assigned task in the [employee portal](/docs/product/employee-portal#my-tasks) can now attach evidence to it. The task description and comment editors have the same paperclip as the console, and files can be dropped or pasted in. Attached files open with a click.

To give files a place to live, assigned employees can now edit the task description. Renaming, reassigning and changing linked controls stay with the task owner.
