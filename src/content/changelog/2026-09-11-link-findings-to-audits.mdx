Until now, a finding stood on its own. If it came out of an audit, you had to track that connection somewhere else, and the report reference that backed it up lived in a different screen.

Findings can now be linked to specific audits. The finding details page has a two-step linking flow, and once linked, the audit shows up with its report references. Unlinking works in place, so a wrong association is fixed without leaving the page.

It keeps the audit trail honest. When you review a finding, you can see which audit produced it and jump straight to the report reference that supports it.
