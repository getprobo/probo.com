When a visitor signs the NDA on your [compliance portal](/docs/product/compliance-portal/visitor-access-and-nda), their card already gave you the certificate of completion. It now offers the signed document as well.

The download menu on the visitor card opens either file: the certificate, or the agreement exactly as the visitor signed it.

On the console GraphQL API, the `ElectronicSignature` type adds an `nda` field with the signed document.
