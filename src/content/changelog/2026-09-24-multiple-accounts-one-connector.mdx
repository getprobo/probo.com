title: "Multiple Accounts per Connector"
description: "Teams can connect additional AWS, GCP, and Azure accounts under one set of credentials, then discover and enable the accounts they need. This makes large, multi-account access reviews easier to set up and maintain."
date: 2026-09-24
images: ["/changelog/2026-09-24-multiple-accounts-one-connector-1.png"]
tags: ["Access Review"]
---

A connector no longer has to stop at the first account. Set it up with one account, then discover and enable the others under the same credential for AWS, GCP, and Azure.

That makes multi-account access reviews easier to grow without repeating the credential setup for every account.
