Uploading [evidence](/docs/product/compliance-program) is only half the job. The other half is documenting what each file actually proves. That takes time, especially when you're uploading screenshots and exports in bulk during an audit prep sprint.

Now when you upload evidence, Probo automatically analyzes the file and generates a plain-language description of what it contains. A screenshot of your GitHub Actions workflow gets described as exactly that. A Dependabot alerts export gets labeled with the number of alerts and their severity levels.

No more staring at a list of `screenshot_2025-09-14.png` files trying to remember which one was which. Every piece of evidence is immediately searchable and understandable by anyone on your team, or by your auditor.
