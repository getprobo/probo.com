Run an [access review](/products/access-review) against your analytics stack, your internal BI tool, your observability layer, or your AI coding environment. Who has access? Who should?

Probo pulls the member list, you confirm who still needs it.

Connectors now support HTTP Basic auth for [API-key connections](/docs/product/access-review/overview). If your tool authenticates with a username/password pair rather than a bearer token, that works now.

Pending signature requests are cancelled automatically when a contract ends or a connector is deactivated. No more ghost signature requests sitting open after you've already closed the loop.
