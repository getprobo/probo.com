Cookie banner tracker measurement now runs in a start-to-stop sample window. Each banner can have one open sample at a time, and the header shows the page loads included in the current window.

Tracker cards rate each pattern against the page loads where it may fire, split by whether consent allowed it. Past discovery counts are not carried into a new sample.
