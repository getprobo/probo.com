This release adds OVHcloud access reviews for EU regions. Connect with authorization code or client credentials, then review local users, the account owner, service accounts, classic API credentials, federated identities, and audit-log activity such as last login and MFA method.

The roster also distinguishes support-created credentials and marks unresolved group roles as unknown rather than assuming they are non-admin. Reviewers get a more complete picture of who can access an OVHcloud account.
