title: "Origin Details on Consent Records"
description: "Consent records now show the origin of the request that created them. Teams get a clearer audit trail when reviewing where consent activity came from across their sites and deployments."
date: 2026-10-05
images: ["/changelog/2026-10-05-consent-record-origins-1.png"]
tags: ["Cookie Banner"]
---

Each consent record now stores and displays the origin of the request. That gives reviewers a direct clue about which site or deployment the consent came from.

The origin is available in the console and in the CLI and n8n consent-record views, keeping it visible wherever teams review consent activity.
