The [Probo SCIM bridge](/docs/product/scim/google-workspace) now supports Google Cloud Identity alongside Google Workspace.

Previously, only Google Workspace accounts could sync users via SCIM. Teams using Google Cloud Identity as their identity provider had to manage users manually.

Now both are supported. The setup flow is the same — connect your Google directory, and Probo handles the rest.

If your organization uses Cloud Identity (common in GCP-native setups without a Workspace license), you can now automate user provisioning and deprovisioning directly.
