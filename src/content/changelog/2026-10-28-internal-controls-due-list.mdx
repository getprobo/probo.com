The internal controls list now puts each control's code, owner, implementation status, and next evidence or test due date in one row. Search by name or code, then filter across statuses or owners.

Sort by the next due date or open a Needs attention view. The list state stays in the URL so you can share a filtered view. As-of snapshots do not contain owners or due dates, so those filters are unavailable there.
