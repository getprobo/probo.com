When you publish your [cookie banner](/products/cookie-banner), Probo now generates a tracker policy document automatically. It lists every tracker type detected on your site, names the vendor, and links back to the relevant third party in your Probo workspace. The website origin appears in the document title so multi-domain setups stay organized.

A background worker keeps the document current: every time a new banner snapshot is published, the policy regenerates. You no longer need to maintain a separate privacy policy section by hand.

From the tracker list, each row now links directly to the generated document. The Display tab on the [banner configuration page](/docs/product/cookie-banner/overview) also moves to the top.
