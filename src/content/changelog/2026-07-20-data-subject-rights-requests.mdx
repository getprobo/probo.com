Data subjects can now file their own [privacy rights requests](/docs/product/compliance-portal/rights-requests) straight from the [Compliance Portal](/products/compliance-portal), no email chains required.

After signing in with a magic link, a visitor can submit a request scoped to their verified email, covering GDPR and CCPA types like access, rectification, objection, and complaints. They can track its status from the same portal instead of asking your team for updates.

For your compliance team, that means fewer one-off emails to track down and a paper trail that lives where the rest of your compliance work already does.
