Comments now follow a linked task between Probo and [Linear](/docs/developers/api/mcp/tools/catalog/linear). Linking or publishing copies the comments already on the task, then later edits and deletions carry across too.

A Linear comment from someone who is not a Probo member is kept without a Probo owner. The discussion stays attached to the work instead of splitting across two timelines.
