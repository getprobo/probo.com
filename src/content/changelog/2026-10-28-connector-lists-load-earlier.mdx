The Integrations and [Access Review](/docs/product/access-review/overview) source pages render before every connector has reported its status. Cards fill in their connection state as vendors respond, rather than holding the whole page until the last result arrives.

Search and other filters remain available while statuses load. A filter that depends on status waits for those values, so it uses the same state the cards show.
