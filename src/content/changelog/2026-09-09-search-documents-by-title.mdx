Finding one document in a long list used to mean scrolling or squinting at a filter dropdown. Now there is a search box right above the document list. Type part of a title and the list narrows down as you go.

The search term is debounced, so it waits a beat before filtering instead of firing on every keystroke, and it is persisted in the URL. That means you can share a link to a filtered view, or hit back and land right where you left off.

It is a small addition, but it is the kind of thing you reach for constantly once a workspace has more than a handful of documents.
