title: "Major.Minor Document Versioning"
description: "Minor version updates no longer require everyone to sign the document again, saving re-signatures for changes that actually matter."
date: 2026-03-31
image: "/changelog/2026-03-31-document-versioning.png"
tags: ["Documents"]
---

Until now, every document version was a major version. Publish a new version, everyone has to sign again. Even for a typo fix.

Documents now support major and minor versions. Publish a minor version and existing signatures stay valid. No re-signing needed. Save that for when the content actually changes in a meaningful way.

Major versions still work the same. Bump the major number and everyone goes through the [approval and signature flow](/docs/product/document-management) again. You decide which one fits.
