[Access review campaigns](/docs/product/access-review/campaigns) just got two new ways to slice the entry list. You can filter by authentication method, so SSO and password based accounts are easy to tell apart, and by account status: Active, Disabled, or Unknown.

Both sit in the campaign toolbar next to the MFA filter that was already there. If you're reviewing a large campaign and only care about disabled accounts still holding access, or password based accounts that skipped SSO, you don't have to scroll past everyone else to find them.
