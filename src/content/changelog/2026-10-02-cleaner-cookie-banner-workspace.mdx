Cookie Banner Discovery has separate pages for Trackers and [Resources](/docs/product/cookie-banner/blocking-resources). Discovery and Trail no longer carry the banner header or publish actions. Those controls stay on Configure.

The lists keep filters, pagination, and sorting in the URL, so a view can be shared as a link. Tracker settings and detections now live on the tracker detail page instead of inline row forms.
