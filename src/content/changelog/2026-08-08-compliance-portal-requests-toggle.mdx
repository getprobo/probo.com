Not every portal wants visitors filing [data subject requests](/docs/product/compliance-portal/rights-requests) through a public page. Now you can turn the Requests surface off entirely from the console overview.

When it's off, the portal drops the nav item and the /requests route returns not found instead of a broken or half-visible page.

The [prb CLI](/docs/developers/cli/commands/compliance-portal) got the same control a day earlier with a rights requests flag on compliance-portal update. This release brings it to the console, where most admins will actually flip it.
