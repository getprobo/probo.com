Probo now supports [Microsoft 365 as a SCIM identity source](/docs/product/scim/microsoft-365). If your team runs on Entra ID (formerly Azure AD), you can connect it directly and have users and groups sync into Probo automatically.

Until now, automated identity sync was limited to Google Workspace customers. Microsoft 365 shops had to manage users manually. The [SCIM bridge](/docs/product/scim/overview) closes that gap.

Once connected, Probo stays in sync with your directory. New hires, role changes, departures — they flow through without any manual work on your end.
