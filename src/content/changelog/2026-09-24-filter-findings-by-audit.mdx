The [findings list](/docs/product/audits-and-findings) now has an audit filter, so reviewers can narrow a busy register to the items tied to one engagement. The same filter is available in the console and through the GraphQL and [MCP interfaces](/docs/developers/api/mcp/tools/catalog/audits-and-findings).

That keeps audit-specific review focused without changing the underlying finding records.
