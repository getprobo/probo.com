---
title: "Cookies stay split by domain"
description: "A cookie name can appear on several hosts with different scopes. Probo now records each cookie's Domain and keeps stronger detection sources, so host-only and domain-wide cookies stay distinct instead of being merged."
date: 2026-10-08
tags: ["Cookie Banner"]
---

Two cookies with the same name can belong to different scopes. Probo now records the cookie Domain and separates detections by scope.

Later, weaker observations no longer replace a stronger source. Tracker records stay distinct and keep useful provenance.
