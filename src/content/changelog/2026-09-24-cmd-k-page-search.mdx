Probo has a lot of pages now. The side nav gets you there, but you still need to know which section a thing lives under, and that takes a few clicks every time.

Hit Cmd+K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) and start typing. You get a list of the pages your account can actually open, narrowed as you type. Pages you don't have access to never show up.

Small feature, but it adds up once you're jumping between audits, findings, and settings in the same session. Works from anywhere in the console, no mouse required.
