images: ["/changelog/2026-08-08-custom-watermark-text-on-exports-1.png"]

Export watermarks used to be locked to the downloader's email address. Now you can type whatever text you want, up to 64 characters.

Want a watermark that reads "Confidential, do not distribute" instead of someone's inbox? Go for it. Exports that already rely on the email watermark keep working exactly as before, this is additive.
