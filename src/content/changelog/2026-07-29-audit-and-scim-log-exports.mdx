Until now, getting your [audit log](/docs/product/audit-log) or [SCIM event history](/docs/product/scim/overview) out of Probo meant asking us for it. Now you can request a CSV export straight from the console, Connect, MCP, or the CLI.

The export runs as a background job. A worker streams the rows and uploads the finished file to S3, so it works the same way whether you're pulling a week of activity or a year's worth.

Useful for auditors who want the raw log outside the app, or for teams piping activity into their own SIEM.
