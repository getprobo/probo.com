---
title: "CCPA Privacy Choices Panel"
description: "California visitors get a Your Privacy Choices panel to opt out of data sale and sharing, plus a notice-only banner where no consent law applies."
date: 2026-08-04
tags: ["Cookie Banner"]
---

CCPA visitors now see a "Your Privacy Choices" panel instead of a generic [cookie banner](/products/cookie-banner). It gives them a direct way to opt out of the sale and sharing of their personal information, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information, matching what the law actually asks for.

We also added a [notice-only banner](/docs/product/cookie-banner/geolocation) for places with no cookie consent law on the books. Instead of forcing an accept/reject choice that doesn't apply, visitors just get a heads-up.
