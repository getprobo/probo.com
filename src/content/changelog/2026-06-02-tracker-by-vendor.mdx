You can now see the vendor behind each tracker directly on the banner trackers page.

[Trackers detected on your site](/docs/product/cookie-banner/overview) are matched to [known third-party vendors](/docs/product/third-party-management). The trackers list shows the linked vendor alongside each tracker, and you can filter the entire list by third party — so if you want to audit everything Salesforce, Stripe, or any other vendor is placing, that's now one click.

This is part of the broader tracker attribution system: when a tracker matches a known pattern, it gets promoted to a vendor automatically. The enrichment runs in the background, so your trackers page stays current without any manual work.

**What changed**

- Tracker list shows the linked third-party vendor per tracker
- New filter to scope the trackers page to a specific vendor
- Backend enrichment worker attributes unmatched trackers to known vendors using catalog data
