Probo already knows which tools you use, since they're in your third-party register. The integrations page now uses that list to suggest the matching connectors, both in the connectors list and in the marketplace.

A banner lists the vendors you could connect. In the marketplace, a lightbulb marks each one that is already in your third-party list but not connected yet, and the Suggested filter shows only those.
