The console and the [compliance portal](/products/compliance-portal) now support French.

Switch your language in account settings and every screen follows along, from the console dashboard to the compliance portal page your customers browse. It is the first language we ship besides English, built on a proper translation setup instead of a handful of translated strings bolted on.

More languages are coming.
