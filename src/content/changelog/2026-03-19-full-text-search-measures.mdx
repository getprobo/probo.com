Finding the right measure used to mean scrolling through long lists or remembering exact names. Not anymore.

You can now search across all your [measures](/docs/product/compliance-program) by keyword. Titles, descriptions, and details are all indexed. Type a few characters and results appear instantly, making it easy to find and link the right measure to your controls.
