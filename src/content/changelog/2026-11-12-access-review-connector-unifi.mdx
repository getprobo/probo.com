[UniFi](/docs/product/access-review/unifi) is now an access review connector. It uses a Site Manager API key and pulls two lists: the administrators of a console's Network application, and the users of its built-in RADIUS server.

If your office Wi-Fi authenticates through UniFi RADIUS, that's the account list auditors ask about. It now goes into the same campaign as the rest of your tools.
