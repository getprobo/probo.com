Consent records are easier to inspect in context. Open one from the trail to see its action, request, TCF string, categories, and location together.

The location view maps the record within its country, including state or province boundaries for the US and Canada. Use previous and next to move through the trail, or switch among recent banner versions.

Cookie banners now have their own CMP section, opening on the latest banner by default.
