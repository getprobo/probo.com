Your [compliance portal](/products/compliance-portal) now has its own Updates section.

Visitors get a paginated list of updates plus a detail page for each one. They can also [subscribe to your mailing list](/docs/product/compliance-portal/updates-and-mailing-list) right from the feed, so they hear about changes without checking back manually.

This gives you a clean way to communicate compliance news without burying it in a document or a one-off email.
