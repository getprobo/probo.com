The riskAssessment resource is live in [n8n](/docs/developers/api/n8n/overview).

It covers the entire risk assessment hierarchy: assessments, scopes, nodes, processes, threats, and scenarios — all with CRUD operations. You can link and unlink scenarios to risks and threats, and retrieve a Mermaid data-flow diagram for any scope.

If you're already automating compliance work through n8n, you can now wire [risk modeling](/docs/product/risk-management) into those same workflows. Trigger risk assessment creation from external events, sync scenario data to other systems, or pull diagrams on a schedule.

No breaking changes. New resource, new operations.
