Probo now supports [access reviews](/products/access-review). Periodically verify that the right people still have the right access.

[Access reviews](/docs/product/access-review/overview) are a core requirement for SOC 2 and ISO 27001. Instead of doing them in spreadsheets, you can run them inside Probo, track who reviewed what, and keep the evidence in one place.
