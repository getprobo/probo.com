A vendor contract rarely stops at a DPA. There is usually an MSA, the terms of service, and an amendment or two. Until now a third party had one slot for a business associate agreement and one for a data privacy agreement.

The [Agreements](/docs/product/third-party-management#agreements) page of a third party now holds as many as you need. Each agreement has a type (master service, terms, data privacy, business associate, amendment, annex or other), a title, optional validity dates and one PDF up to 30 MB. Every row can be previewed, downloaded, edited and deleted, and the add dialog shows the PDF before you upload it.

Existing BAAs and DPAs were moved to the new list automatically. The published third-party list still fills its BAA and DPA columns, from the newest agreement of each type.

Also available from MCP, `prb third-party agreement` and n8n. MCP can't set the amendment or annex types yet.

Heads up: the GraphQL API replaces the business associate and data privacy agreement fields and mutations with `agreements`, `uploadThirdPartyAgreement`, `updateThirdPartyAgreement` and `deleteThirdPartyAgreement`. The old n8n agreement operations are removed, so update workflows that use them.
