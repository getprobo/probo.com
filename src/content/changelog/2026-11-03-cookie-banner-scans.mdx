A [cookie banner](/docs/product/cookie-banner/overview) can now scan its own site. Request a scan from the console and Probo crawls the site, recording the cookies, storage, and resources each page sets. The scan page shows its progress, the pages it visited, and everything it found.

You no longer have to wait for visitors to hit a page before its trackers show up. Run a scan after you add a new script or launch a new section, and categorise what it finds before anyone visits.

The tracker list has new views to match: categorised, uncategorised, from visitors, from scan, and all. A Seen by column tells you who reported each tracker, and you can filter on it. Each detection also shows its cookie details.

Scans are available from the API, MCP, `prb`, and n8n too.
