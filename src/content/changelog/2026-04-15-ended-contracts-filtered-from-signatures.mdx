---
title: "Ended Contracts Filtered from Signatures"
description: "People with ended contracts no longer appear in the signature request dialog, so former employees don't clutter the list."
date: 2026-04-15
tags: ["Documents"]
---

When you [send a document for signature](/docs/product/document-management), the dialog now only shows people with active contracts. Former employees and ended contracts are filtered out automatically.

Saves you from scrolling past people who left and accidentally sending requests to the wrong list.
