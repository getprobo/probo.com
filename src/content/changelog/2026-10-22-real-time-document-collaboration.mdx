The document editor now works on the live document. Other editors appear in a facepile with a live caret, so concurrent edits are visible as they happen.

A history panel groups edits into sessions by author, highlights inserted and deleted text, and lets you restore a session from the draft. Publishing or requesting approval creates a version, and its link remains a frozen snapshot.
