Sometimes someone leaves. You want them out of active workflows but not erased from history.

Probo now has an archiveUser operation. Archive a user and they're deactivated — removed from active processes, access reviews, signature requests — but still in the org for audit trail purposes. Their pending invitations are automatically expired.

Available across the console UI, MCP, [CLI](/docs/developers/cli/commands/user), and [n8n](/docs/developers/api/n8n/resources/user). No more choosing between "keep everything" and "delete and lose context."

IAM policies updated: owners get full iam:scim-bridge:\* access, admins get read-only SCIM bridge access.
