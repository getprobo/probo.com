Half-written comments on a [task](/docs/product/tasks/overview#comments-and-activity) no longer disappear when you click away.

If the comment composer holds text, an attachment, or an upload that is still in progress, leaving the task page asks you to confirm first. This applies in the console and the employee portal. Closing the browser tab shows the browser's own prompt.

Changing the search on the same task does not ask, because the draft stays in place.
