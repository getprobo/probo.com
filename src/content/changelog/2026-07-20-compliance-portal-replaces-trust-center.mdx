Trust Center is gone. The [Compliance Portal](/products/compliance-portal) is live in production for the /trust path and on [custom domains](/docs/product/compliance-portal/branding-and-domains), replacing the old frontend for good.

Visitors now sign in with a magic link or their own OAuth or OIDC provider, and the sign-in screen shows your logo and name instead of a generic form. The home page displays your full custom portal title, comes with reorderable commitment cards, and every organization gets a managed domain and certificate on probopage.com by default, no DNS work required.

The documents page got an upgrade too. Visitors can browse by category, filter by public or private visibility, and open PDFs, images, and other files right in the browser. The portal now speaks eleven more languages, including German, Spanish, Japanese, and Korean.

If you had bookmarks or integrations pointing at the old Trust Center, check the naming. Trust Center is Compliance Portal everywhere now, in the console, the API, and the docs.
