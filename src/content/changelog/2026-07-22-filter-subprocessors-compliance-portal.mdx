Your [Compliance Portal](/products/compliance-portal) subprocessor list just got a filter.

Visitors can now [filter subprocessors by facet](/docs/product/compliance-portal/subprocessors) directly on the public trust page. No more scrolling through a long list to find what matters to them.

The filtering runs server-side, so it stays fast even with a large subprocessor list.
