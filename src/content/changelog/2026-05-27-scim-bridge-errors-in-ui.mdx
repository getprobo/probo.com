description: "Sync errors from the SCIM bridge are now exposed in the API and surfaced on the Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 connector cards in the console."
date: 2026-05-27
tags: ["IAM"]
---

When your [SCIM bridge](/docs/product/scim/overview) has a sync problem, you'll now know about it before it becomes a people management incident.

Sync errors from the SCIM bridge are now exposed directly in the Probo API and surfaced on the Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 connector cards in the console. No more digging through logs to find out why a sync silently stopped working.

The [MCP API](/docs/developers/api/mcp/tools/catalog/identity-and-provisioning) also now exposes the profile source field on users — useful for automation that needs to distinguish SCIM-provisioned users from manually created ones.
