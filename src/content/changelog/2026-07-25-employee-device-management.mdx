Employees can now [enroll their work devices](/docs/product/device-agent/install) in Probo through [a new agent called probo-agent](/docs/product/device-agent/overview). Once enrolled, admins see the whole device fleet in the console: who owns each device, what its posture looks like, and when it last checked in.

Admins can assign or reassign device owners directly from the console. No more chasing down who has which laptop in a spreadsheet.

Employees get their own side of this too. They can confirm their device is enrolled and check its status without an admin generating a session for them first. That was a manual step before. Now it's self-service.
