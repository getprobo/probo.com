You can now link documents to measures. If a policy supports a specific [control measure](/docs/product/compliance-program), connect them directly.

This tightens the relationship between your [documentation](/docs/product/document-management) and your compliance controls. When an auditor asks "where's the evidence for this measure?", the linked documents are right there.
