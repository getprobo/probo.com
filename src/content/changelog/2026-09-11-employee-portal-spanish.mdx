title: "Spanish for the Employee Portal"
description: "The employee portal now ships in Spanish, joining English, French, and Dutch, so team members can review signatures, approvals, and documents in the language they work in."
date: 2026-09-11
tags: ["Internationalization"]
---

The employee portal now speaks Spanish. It joins English, French, and Dutch as the languages the portal ships in.

For teams with Spanish-speaking members, that means signatures, approvals, and document reviews read naturally instead of forcing everyone into one language. The interface, labels, and flows all follow the same translation set as the rest of the product.
