Probo now runs an [IP-to-country geolocation service](/docs/product/cookie-banner/geolocation) on every [cookie banner](/products/cookie-banner) interaction.

When a visitor lands on your site, Probo identifies their country and maps it to the applicable privacy regulation — GDPR, UK GDPR, FADP, CCPA, PIPEDA, LGPD, LFPDPPP, POPIA, PDPA, PIPL, PIPA, APPI, DPDP, or PDPL.

That regulation is then stored on the consent record.

The banner adapts accordingly: CCPA visitors see an opt-out notice. Visitors from regions with no applicable regulation see a simple notice. Visitors under GDPR see the standard consent flow.

The regulation and country code are now available across GraphQL, MCP, CLI, and n8n — so you can filter consent records by jurisdiction in audits or reporting pipelines.

No configuration needed. It works automatically for all new consent events.
