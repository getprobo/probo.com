**Probo Cloud** is the managed offering. Probo hosts the platform, ships updates, and operates backups and security controls. It is intended for teams that want to use Probo without operating the underlying infrastructure.

## Regions

Choose a data region when your organization is onboarded:

| Region | Console                              |
| ------ | ------------------------------------ |
| **EU** | [eu.probo.com](https://eu.probo.com) |
| **US** | [us.probo.com](https://us.probo.com) |

Your organization’s data is stored in the selected region. Customer organizations are logically isolated on shared regional infrastructure.

## What Probo operates

- application hosting and platform updates;
- regional data storage and backups;
- infrastructure monitoring and maintenance;
- network, encryption, and secret-management controls.

## Shared responsibility

| Probo                                                              | Your organization                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hosting, updates, backups, infrastructure security, and monitoring | Users, roles, SSO/SCIM setup, integrations, compliance content, and evidence |
| Protecting the managed platform and its service credentials        | Deciding which integrations to connect and who has administrative access     |

Contractual availability, recovery, retention, and security details are provided through customer agreements and the [Compliance Portal](https://compliance.probo.com).

## Cloud vs self-hosting

|                                 | **Probo Cloud**            | **Self-hosted**                  |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Who operates infrastructure** | Probo                      | Your team                        |
| **Upgrades**                    | Managed by Probo           | Managed by your team             |
| **Region choice**               | EU or US                   | Any hosting location you control |
| **Best when**                   | You want a managed service | You need full control of hosting |
