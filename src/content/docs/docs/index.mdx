Probo is an open-source governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) platform for engineering, security, and compliance teams. It brings together requirements, controls, evidence, risks, audits, and the information you share with customers. Start a program, find the steps for a task, or connect Probo to your own tools.

## Explore Probo

New to the platform? The [product overview](/docs/product/getting-started/product-overview) explains how engineering, security, and compliance teams work together in Probo.

Already know where you are starting?

- **Cloud customer:** review [Probo Cloud](/docs/deployment/cloud), including regions, shared responsibility, and onboarding.
- **Self-host operator:** start with [self-hosting](/docs/deployment/self-hosting), then choose Docker Compose or Kubernetes.
- **Integrator:** use the [developer guides](/docs/developers) to choose an interface and authenticate.
