Probo documents are controlled records for policies, procedures, reports, and other compliance material. A document has a lifecycle; its versions preserve the content and decisions that were current at a point in time.

## Versions and publication

Edit work in a draft version. Publishing creates the version intended for use or review. Archived documents remain part of the record but are removed from active use. Avoid editing a document merely to change its approval history; create a new version when the controlled content changes.

To share selected documents outside your workspace, configure their portal visibility separately. See [Publishing content in the Compliance Portal](/docs/product/compliance-portal/publishing-content).

## Review with comments and suggestions

Members can review a draft in place, from the **Description** tab of the document:

- **Comments:** select a passage, click **Comment**, and write a note. Comments support rich text and member mentions. Anyone who can comment can reply, **Resolve** a thread, or **Reopen** it later. Only the author of a comment can edit or delete it.
- **Suggestions:** switch on **Suggesting** to record your edits as redlines instead of changing the document. A reviewer with the right permission accepts or rejects each suggestion in place.

Suggesting mode tracks these changes:

| Change           | What is tracked                                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Text             | Insertions, deletions, and removed line breaks                                                                    |
| Character styles | Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, code, and links. Readers see the previous formatting until it is accepted |
| Blocks           | Headings, lists and list nesting, quotes, code blocks, dividers, images, files, and mentions                      |
| Tables           | Inserting or deleting a row, a column, or a whole table                                                           |

Accepting a suggestion keeps the new text, style, or block. Rejecting it restores what was there before, including the previous formatting. Each margin card describes the change in words, such as `Add: "…"`, `Delete: "…"`, `Format: bold`, or `Add row: with text "…"`.

Row suggestions are refused on tables with merged cells. Any other table structure edit made while suggesting is dropped with a message, rather than saved without a redline.

Review cards sit in the margin next to the passage they refer to and stay in sync across open tabs. Viewers and auditors can comment and suggest without being able to edit the document. Only owners and admins can accept or reject suggestions; see [Roles and permissions](/docs/product/roles-and-permissions).

Members with read-only access open the same live document as editors, so they see other people's edits as they happen. Probo rejects any content change sent from a read-only session. To propose a change, a reviewer leaves a comment or switches on **Suggesting**.

The document stays editable while suggestions are open. A direct edit outside a suggestion is applied; an edit that lands on an open suggestion is refused. You cannot publish the document, or restore an earlier version, until every suggestion is accepted or rejected.

Comments and suggestions are also available through the GraphQL API, the [MCP server](/docs/developers/api/mcp/tools/catalog/documents-and-approvals), and the [n8n node](/docs/developers/api/n8n/resources/document-suggestion). An automation can start a suggestion from a quoted passage and its replacement, then add text, format, or table redlines to it. The `changeDocumentText`, `changeDocumentFormat`, `changeDocumentTable`, and `changeDocumentBlock` tools either apply a change directly (`APPLY`) or record it on an open suggestion (`SUGGEST`), without sending editor JSON.

## Approval quorums

An approval quorum defines who must decide before a document can proceed. Decisions are recorded against a specific version so later revisions do not rewrite the history of an earlier approval.

## Electronic signatures

Signature requests also belong to a document version. A request can be completed, cancelled, or voided according to its state. Confirm the signer identity and intended document version before requesting signatures.

## Export and evidence

Documents can be exported as PDF and linked to controls and internal controls. A controlled document explains what the organization intends to do; evidence demonstrates that an internal control operated. Keeping those roles distinct makes audit review clearer.

Access to drafts, approvals, and signatures follows organization authorization. Portal visitors use a separate access workflow for restricted published documents; see [Visitor access and NDA](/docs/product/compliance-portal/visitor-access-and-nda). Use the audit log when investigating changes to controlled records. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/product/roles-and-permissions).
