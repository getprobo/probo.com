Your first goal is a small, working compliance program: the right people, one framework, an internal control, and evidence that supports it. You can expand from there.

If you are still deciding, [choose a deployment](/docs/product/getting-started/choose-deployment). Cloud is managed by Probo; a production self-hosted installation needs an operator responsible for its infrastructure and maintenance.

## Recommended path

<Steps>

1. **Understand what you are setting up**

   Read the [product overview](/docs/product/getting-started/product-overview) to see what Probo covers and how your team will use it.

2. **Get access to an instance**

   [Choose a deployment](/docs/product/getting-started/choose-deployment). Follow [Cloud first steps](/docs/product/getting-started/cloud-first-steps) for the managed service or the [self-hosted quickstart](/docs/product/getting-started/quickstart) for a local evaluation.

3. **Build your first program**

   [Set up your organization and first framework](/docs/product/getting-started/first-organization-and-framework): invite teammates, review controls, and collect your first evidence.

4. **Connect the concepts to your work**

   Use [Core concepts](/docs/product/getting-started/core-concepts) while working with requirements, internal controls, evidence, and risks. You do not need to learn every record type before starting.

</Steps>
