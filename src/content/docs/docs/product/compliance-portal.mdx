The Compliance Portal is a branded place for customers and prospects to review your security posture. Choose what to share from your Probo organization, control access to sensitive resources, and keep the information connected to your compliance program.

Nothing is shared automatically. You choose which frameworks, reports, documents, commitments, references, and subprocessors to publish, what requires approval, and whether visitors must sign an NDA before exporting protected documents.

## Choose what customers can see

The portal uses information already managed in Probo. Select the approved items you want to share:

- Frameworks and audit reports that show your compliance coverage
- Policies, procedures, and other documents
- Security commitments and supporting files
- Customer references and useful external links
- Subprocessors selected from your third-party inventory

Adding or updating information in Probo does not automatically expose it. Each portal has its own selection of published content.

## Protect sensitive documents

For each document, audit report, or portal file, choose how it is shared:

- **Not published** — it stays inside Probo and does not appear on the portal.
- **Public** — anyone visiting the portal can open it.
- **Restricted** — visitors can see that it exists, but must sign in, request access, and receive your approval before opening it.

You can also add an NDA to the portal. Visitors may request access before signing it, but they must complete the electronic signature before exporting protected documents.

## Operations and administration

Keep the portal useful after launch: maintain its content, review who has access, and verify its public presentation.

- [Updates and mailing list](/docs/product/compliance-portal/updates-and-mailing-list) — publish compliance updates and manage subscribers.
- [Rights requests](/docs/product/compliance-portal/rights-requests) — let visitors submit and track privacy requests.
- [Multiple portals](/docs/product/compliance-portal/multiple-portals) — operate separate publishing surfaces for entities, products, or audiences.
- [SEO and discoverability](/docs/product/compliance-portal/seo-and-discoverability) — control indexing and review machine-readable portal content.
- [Permissions and integrations](/docs/product/compliance-portal/permissions-and-integrations) — understand organization roles, Slack notifications, and automation access.
