Each Compliance Portal has its own mailing list. Visitors can subscribe to receive compliance updates, and portal managers can add or remove subscribers from the console.

## Mailing list settings

Open the portal, then select **Updates**. The **Settings** card at the top configures how your mailing list behaves.

- **Reply-to email**: the address that receives replies when a subscriber answers an update email, for example `security@yourcompany.com`. Use a monitored inbox so customer questions reach your security team.

## Get subscribers

People join the mailing list in one of two ways:

- **They subscribe themselves.** A visitor signs in to your Compliance Portal and selects **Subscribe to updates**.
- **You add them.** Open the portal, then select **Updates** → **Subscribers** → **Add subscriber** and enter their email address. Use this when a person has asked to join outside the portal. Do not add people without an appropriate basis for contacting them.

In both cases, the new subscriber must confirm by email before they receive any update.

## Confirm a subscription

<Steps>

1. **Subscribe**

   In the **Subscribe to updates** dialog, the visitor enters their email address and selects **Subscribe**. The dialog states that they agree to receive compliance updates from your company and can unsubscribe at any time.

2. **Confirm by email**

   Probo sends a **Confirm your subscription** email. The subscriber selects **Confirm subscription**.

3. **Subscription confirmed**

   A **Subscription confirmed** page tells the subscriber they will now receive updates. Their status changes from **Pending** to **Confirmed**.

</Steps>

Subscribers you add from the console go through the same email confirmation.

## Manage subscribers

Open the portal, then select **Updates** and the **Subscribers** tab. The subscriber list shows each person's name, email address, subscription status, and subscription date.

- **Pending** subscribers have not completed confirmation and do not receive updates.
- **Confirmed** subscribers receive updates.
- Removing a subscriber stops future sends to that address.

## Create and send an update

<Steps>

1. Open **Updates** and stay on the **Updates** tab.
2. Select **Add update** and enter a title and body.
3. Review the draft for confidential or customer-specific information.
4. Select **Send**, review the confirmation, and confirm the send.

</Steps>

A new update is saved with the **Draft** status. Each draft in the list has three actions:

- **Send**: opens the **Send update to subscribers** confirmation, which shows the title and body that will be sent to all current subscribers.
- **Edit**: changes the title or body before sending.
- **Delete**: removes the draft.

Sending queues the update for delivery and cannot be undone. A sent update becomes read-only; create a new update if you need to issue a correction. Delivery also requires working outbound email configuration.

{/* Screenshot: /docs/compliance-portal/send-update.webp — Draft compliance update and send confirmation */}

## Visitor experience

Sent updates appear in the portal's **Updates** section as a news feed. Signed-in visitors can choose **Subscribe to updates** and unsubscribe later. A subscription affects email delivery only; visitors can still read published updates without subscribing.

{/* Screenshot: /docs/compliance-portal/updates-feed.webp — Public Updates section and subscription action */}

## Automate mailing-list work

The [Compliance Portal MCP tools](/docs/developers/api/mcp/tools/catalog/compliance-portal#getMailingList) can list subscribers, create and edit drafts, and queue sends. Treat `sendMailingListUpdate` as an external side effect and require an explicit review step before calling it.
