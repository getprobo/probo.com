The Compliance Portal can publish selected vendors from your organization’s third-party inventory. Use this catalog to tell visitors which external organizations process or host data for your service.

## Prepare the inventory

Create and maintain third-party records under [Third-party management](/docs/product/third-party-management) before publishing them. The portal uses the source record’s:

- Name
- Category
- Description
- Website URL
- Countries

Only top-level third parties are available in the portal picker. Complete the public-facing fields before adding a record.

## Add a subprocessor

<Steps>

1. Open **Compliance Portals** and select the portal.
2. Open **Subprocessors**.
3. Select **Add subprocessor**.
4. Choose an available third party.
5. Confirm the addition.

</Steps>

The subprocessor appears on the public portal immediately when the portal is active. There is no restricted visibility option for this catalog.

## Visitor experience

Visitors can search the catalog and filter it by category and country. Entries are grouped by category and show the vendor name, description, website-derived logo, and listed countries.

{/* Screenshot placeholder: Public subprocessor catalog — /docs/compliance-portal/subprocessors-catalog.webp */}

## Update or remove an entry

Edit the underlying third-party record to correct its public details. Remove the entry from the **Subprocessors** page when it should no longer be disclosed on this portal.

Removing a subprocessor from the portal removes only the portal association; it does not delete the source third-party record.

Review the published list whenever your vendor inventory or data-processing relationships change.
