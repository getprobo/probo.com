---
title: PlanetScale
description: Connect PlanetScale as an access review source using OAuth so Probo can list the members of a PlanetScale organization and their roles.
---

Probo reads the members of a PlanetScale organization through the PlanetScale API so you can review who has access. You connect with **OAuth**: you authorize Probo on PlanetScale and tick the organization to review.

:::caution
PlanetScale grants organization permissions only to the organizations you tick on its authorization screen. If you click **Authorize access** without ticking the organization you want to review, the connection succeeds but Probo cannot read that organization's members.
:::

## Prerequisites

- Probo organization administrator access
- Membership in the PlanetScale organization you want to review. Probo reads the member list with the access of the PlanetScale account that authorizes it

PlanetScale has no member status, so Probo shows every member it collects as active. With SSO, a member removed from your identity provider stays in the organization and can no longer sign in until someone removes them in PlanetScale or Directory Sync removes them. Pending invitations are not collected.

## Connect PlanetScale

![PlanetScale authorization screen with an organization ticked and the Read members and Read organization permissions](/docs/access-review/planetscale-authorize.webp)

<Steps>

1. In Probo, go to **Settings** > **Connectors** and open the **Marketplace**.
2. Find **PlanetScale**, enter a **Name** for the connection (e.g. `main`), and click **Connect**.
3. Sign in to PlanetScale if prompted. On the authorization screen, expand **Organizations** and tick the organization you want to review. Probo asks for **Read members** and **Read organization** on that organization, and for read access to your user profile and organization list.
4. Click **Authorize access**. Back in Probo, the PlanetScale connector shows as **Connected**. If you ticked a single organization, Probo selects it.
5. If you ticked more than one organization, open the PlanetScale connector in **Settings** > **Connectors** and choose the organization to review in the **Organization** field.
6. Go to **Access Review** > **Sources**, click **Add sources**, tick **PlanetScale**, and click **Add**.

</Steps>

Probo names the source after the PlanetScale organization and pulls its members into your campaigns.

## Troubleshooting

- **Add sources shows "Pick an organization in Settings first."** Probo has no organization selected for the connector. If you ticked several organizations, choose one in the **Organization** field of the PlanetScale connector in **Settings** > **Connectors**. If that field lists none, you did not tick any organization on PlanetScale's authorization screen: reconnect PlanetScale and tick the organization under **Organizations** before clicking **Authorize access**.
- **An organization you belong to is missing from the Organization field.** Probo lists only the organizations you ticked when you authorized it. Reconnect and tick that organization as well.
- **The source shows as disconnected.** PlanetScale no longer accepts Probo's authorization, for example because an organization administrator opened **Settings** > **OAuth applications** > **Installed** in PlanetScale and clicked **Revoke access** on Probo. Reconnect PlanetScale and authorize Probo again.
- **A former colleague still appears.** A campaign keeps the members it collected when it started. Remove the member from the organization in PlanetScale and the next campaign picks up the change.
