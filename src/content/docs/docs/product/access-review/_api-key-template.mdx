## Step 2: Connect in Probo

<Steps>

1. In Probo, go to **Settings** > **Connectors** and click **Add more** to open the **Marketplace**.
2. Find **<Provider>** and open it.
3. Enter a **Name** for the connection (e.g. `main`), paste the key into **API key**, and click **Connect**.
4. Go to **Access Review** > **Sources**, click **Add sources**, tick **<Provider>**, and click **Add**.
