  🔐 We're now ISO 27001 Certified

  Security and trust have always been core to how we build and operate. Today, we're proud to share an important milestone: we've achieved ISO/IEC 27001 certification, the international standard for information security management system (ISMS).

  This isn't just about passing an audit. It reflects the systems, processes, and culture we've put in place to manage risk, protect sensitive information, and continuously improve how we safeguard our customers' data.

  For our customers and partners, this means:

  - A structured, risk-based approach to information security
  - Strong governance across people, processes, and technology
  - An ongoing commitment to protecting what matters most

  A big thank you to Probo (YC P25), whose platform and team made the entire ISO 27001 journey significantly smoother.

  And of course, huge thanks to everyone internally who contributed to making this happen.
