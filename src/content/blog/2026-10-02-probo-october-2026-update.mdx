## One cookie banner for every privacy law your visitors fall under

Your visitors don't all live under the same privacy law. A banner built for GDPR is wrong for a visitor in California, and the reverse is just as true.

The Probo Cookie Banner works out each visitor's jurisdiction and applies the right rules. Every consent is recorded with an anonymized IP, so you have proof when someone asks. Your cookie policy is generated from your live configuration, which means it stays in sync with what the banner actually does.

It's open source (MIT) and ships in 14 languages.

## Your next audit, without the device screenshots

Collecting laptop screenshots from every employee is the most tedious part of most audits. It doesn't have to be.

With the Device Agent, employees enroll their laptops themselves from the employee portal. From then on, every check is logged with a timestamp, so device evidence is ready when the auditor asks. If you haven't rolled it out yet, now is a good time.
